🆘 What is the SOS Button?
SOS means emergency alert. If you ever feel unsafe during a ride — or any emergency — tap the red SOS button at the top of the screen.

It will instantly send your GPS location to everyone saved in your Emergency Contacts (Profile → Emergency Contacts) via WhatsApp.

Your phone also vibrates 3 times to confirm the alert was sent.

Add at least one emergency contact (parent, sibling, or friend) so SOS works when you need it.

Frequently Asked Questions
How do I book a ride? ›
Enter your pickup location and destination, choose ride type (Okada, Keke, or Goods), select payment method and tap Book Now. No registration needed — just your phone number.
Do I need to register to use the app? ›
No. Just open the app and book immediately. You only need your phone number at booking. No password, no email, no account creation required.
How do I pay? ›
You can pay by Cash (pay rider directly on arrival), Card (Visa, Mastercard, Verve via Paystack), Bank Transfer (all Nigerian banks, Opay, OPay, Kuda), or Wallet (load money in advance).
What goods transport is available? ›
Aboki Keke Cargo, Mini Van/Sienna, Pickup Truck, Cargo Truck/Small (popular in Suleja) for light/medium goods; and Lorry/Bedford, Tipper Truck, Royal Trailer, Flatbed/Low-Loader for heavy goods. Select Goods on the home screen to see all options. Taxi is available in FCT Abuja and parts of Lagos where Okada and Keke are restricted.
How do I become a rider? ›
Go to Profile → Register as a Rider → fill the 4-step form. The owner will contact you on WhatsApp within 24 hours to verify your documents. Once approved, you appear in the app and customers can book you.
Is my payment secure? ›
Yes. All online payments are processed by Paystack, Nigeria's most trusted payment platform. Your card details are never stored on RideNaija servers.
How does live tracking work? ›
After booking, a live map appears showing your current location. When prompted, allow location access. The map updates as you move so the rider can find you easily.
