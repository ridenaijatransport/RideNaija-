📐 How Zone Pricing Works (Auto-determined by the app):

When a customer books a ride, the app compares the pickup and drop-off areas entered as text. It checks if both locations share the same neighbourhood keyword — if yes, it's "Within same area". If not, it estimates distance based on how many distinct area names are mentioned and applies the right zone.

Real example (Suleja):
🟢 Rafin Sanyi → APC Quarters — both are within Suleja town. The app detects no cross-town keywords → Within same area (₦200) or Short distance (₦350) depending on how far apart the text suggests.
🟡 Suleja → Minna — different town names detected → Long distance / Cross town fare applies.

The zone flat rates here OVERRIDE the per-km estimate when a known zone is detected. You can set each zone price to match your local market. Riders' registered routes help customers find them — the pricing zones determine what they pay.

🗺️ State & City Price Multipliers

Adjust fare rates per state. 1.0 = base rate (Niger/Suleja). Lagos = 2.0 means prices are 2× the base. These multiply all vehicle fares automatically.

How multipliers work:
If Okada base fare = ₦150 and Lagos multiplier = 2.0 → Lagos Okada base = ₦300.
Set multipliers to match local market realities in each state. 1.0 = no change. 1.5 = 50% higher. 0.9 = 10% lower.

📅 Dedicated Rider Subscription Manager
What it is: A customer pays a fixed weekly/monthly fee to have a rider always available for their daily route — no need to book each time.
Price logic: Base rate per trip × trips per day × working days in plan. Monthly gets 10% off, School Term gets 15% off. State multiplier applies on top.
Distance variation: The base rate is set per vehicle type. For longer routes than average, use the "Custom Fare Override" when confirming — you can type in a higher per-trip amount that reflects the actual route distance.
Your cut: You collect the full subscription upfront from the customer (via OPay). You then pay your rider their share below. For dedicated riders, rider share is 70% of the total subscription — you keep 30%.
Discount: The discount is already deducted from the customer's price before you even collect. So if a customer owes ₦13,200 after 10% off, that's what they pay. Rider still gets 70% of that final amount. You keep 30%.
