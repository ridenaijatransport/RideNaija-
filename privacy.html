Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information RideNaija ("we", "us") collects when you use our app or website, how we use it, and who we share it with. RideNaija provides delivery, food ordering, and market-run services across Nigeria.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details — your name and phone number, so a rider or vendor can reach you about your order.
  • Location — the pickup and drop-off addresses you enter, and, if you allow it, your live device location so we can show a rider your position during an active order.
  • Order details — items, notes, and delivery instructions you provide for a delivery, food, or market order.
  • Payment information — when you pay by card, bank transfer, or USSD, your payment is processed directly by Paystack, our licensed payment processor. We never see or store your card number or bank details — we only receive confirmation that a payment succeeded.
  • Wallet activity — your in-app wallet balance and transaction history, if you choose to use it.
  • Photos — restaurant and shop partners may upload menu or product photos as part of their listings.

How We Use Your Information

  • To fulfill your order — matching you with a rider or vendor and coordinating pickup/delivery.
  • To process payments and maintain your wallet balance.
  • To contact you about an order in progress, by phone or WhatsApp.
  • To improve the reliability of our service, such as fixing pricing or routing issues.

Who We Share It With

  • Riders and vendors involved in fulfilling your specific order receive only what's needed to complete it — your phone number, pickup/drop-off details, and order contents.
  • Paystack processes any card, transfer, or USSD payment directly. Their handling of your payment data is governed by their own privacy policy.
  • Firebase (Google) hosts our backend database and push notifications. Google processes this data on our behalf under their standard data protection terms.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not share it for advertising purposes.

Your Choices

You can decline to share live location — the app will still work, though a rider won't be able to see your position on the map during delivery. You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time by contacting us below.

Data Retention

We keep order history and wallet transaction records for as long as your account is active, and as needed to resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. You can request deletion at any time.

Children

RideNaija is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
